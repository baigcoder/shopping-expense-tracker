## 1. Brand Philosophy: The Sovereign Financial Terminal

Cashly is neither a playful consumer budgeting toy nor an opaque, sterile enterprise ERP. It is an **editorial financial instrument** created for users who demand absolute clarity over their capital velocity, committed obligations, and forward trajectory.

### The Core Voice Attributes:
- **Unflinching Precision**: Numbers are never rounded casually; data provenance is immutable; calculations are verifiable.
- **Architectural Weight**: Visual components feel physical, solid, and rooted. They do not tremble with frivolous hover effects or neon glows.
- **Editorial Typography**: Large, confident typographic declarations in Syne display sans command immediate visual respect, establishing hierarchy before the eye even reads the numerals.
- **Color with Purpose**: Color is never decorative. Every hue is an operational signal (Cadmium Orange for available headroom and primary triggers; Soft Pink for discretionary velocity; Muted Sage for inflows; Deep Burgundy for contractual locks; Deep Ink for sovereign authority).

---

## 2. Art Direction Rules & Directives

### Rule 1: The Anti-Card Spam Mandate
- Traditional fintech dashboards clutter screens with identical 3x3 grids of white cards featuring 12px rounded borders and small icon badges.
- **Cashly Directive**: Group functional zones into monumental color-blocked blocks, full-bleed data tables with hairline dividers, and asymmetric split panels.

### Rule 2: High Typographic Contrast
- Every view must have an undeniable typographic hero moment (e.g., `COMMAND & CONTROL`, `SOVEREIGN REVIEW TERMINAL`, `LOCKED CAPITAL TIMELINE`).
- Primary headings utilize `editorial-title` in Syne 700/800 with tight letter-spacing (`-0.03em`) and uppercase tracking.
- Numerals utilize monospaced tabular figures (`font-mono tabular-nums`), ensuring mathematical alignment across financial statements.

### Rule 3: The Golden Master Inheritance
- The Landing Page (V10) establishes the canonical art direction for the entire ecosystem.
- Authentication, Dashboard, Activity, Review, Plan, Analyze, Money Twin, Assist, Cards, Extension, Settings, and Mobile all inherit the exact same visual DNA:
  - Same color tokens (`--color-orange`, `--color-pink`, `--color-sage`, `--color-wine`, `--color-ink`, `--color-ivory`).
  - Same pill geometries (`rounded-full`, 9999px border-radius).
  - Same hairline border weights (`border border-ink/10` or `border-white/10`).
  - Same dark-mode console elegance (`bg-[#111111]`).

---

## 3. Brand Assets & Materials

- **Display Typeface**: Syne (Google Fonts, weights 600, 700, 800) for all primary titles and section declarations.
- **Body & Data Typeface**: Plus Jakarta Sans / Inter / SF Pro Display for metadata, supporting body copy, and UI controls.
- **Numerical Typeface**: JetBrains Mono / Geist Mono / standard monospaced tabular numbers for financial amounts, dates, and account identifiers.
- **Canvas Base**: Studio Warm Ivory (`#F4F3EE`) for light mode app surfaces and marketing canvases; Deep Matte Ink (`#111111` / `#0D0E12`) for high-contrast console surfaces.
