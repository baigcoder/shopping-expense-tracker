## 1. Flagship Narrative: "If Nothing Changes..."

Money Twin is Cashly's sovereign predictive simulation engine. It generates an exact financial shadow twin that projects net worth, runway depletion, and end-of-month cash positions under current behavioral inertia.

### The Flagship Declaration:
> *"IF NOTHING CHANGES, THIS IS WHERE YOUR MONTH ENDS."*  
Rendered in monumental Syne extra-bold typography with a Deep Burgundy badge (`#80383D`).
