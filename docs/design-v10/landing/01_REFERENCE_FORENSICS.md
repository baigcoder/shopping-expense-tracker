# 01 — REFERENCE FORENSIC AUDIT (LANDING V10)
## Primary Visual Art-Direction Reference: Layo Finance App Concept (Dribbble)

---

### 1. Executive Summary & Design DNA
The primary visual benchmark is an editorial, high-contrast, tactile fintech concept characterized by:
- **Magazine-Grade Asymmetry**: Large text blocks juxtaposed directly against cropped, high-density product canvases.
- **Bold Color Blocking**: Massive single-tone visual zones using Cadmium Orange, Soft Candy Pink, Muted Sage Green, and Chocolate Burgundy against a warm studio ivory canvas.
- **Tactile 3D Geometric Materials**: Physical-feeling architectural geometry (matte white plaster cubes with ambient occlusion) that grounds the digital product in physical space.
- **Sculptural Display Typography**: Wide extended geometric grotesque letterforms (`Syne` / wide display sans) with uppercase titles, tight leading, and high tracking contrast.
- **Oversized Device Framing**: 44px corner geometry, realistic status bars (`9:41`), hairline dividers, and floating navigation pills that make interfaces feel physical and touchable.

### 3. Translation to Cashly V10
We extract these visual laws and apply them across the entire Cashly product story:
1. **No generic SaaS templates**: No centered 3-card rows with generic icon circles.
2. **Desktop + Mobile + Extension Triptych**: Cashly is presented as a complete ecosystem across all three form factors.
3. **Sticky Lifecycle Narrative**: 6 stages (Capture, Review, Understand, Plan, Predict, Act) presented as giant typographic stages with transforming product visuals.
4. **Interactive Flagship Moments**:
   - Money Twin forecast simulator with live cash runway trajectory.
   - Real-time review queue simulation with Approve/Dismiss actions.
   - Contextual AI action chips grounded in actual transaction data.
