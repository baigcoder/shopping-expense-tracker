# 03 — COMPOSITION & LAYOUT SYSTEM (LANDING V10)

1. **01 Hero**: Poster-scale campaign manifesto with integrated 3D architectural cubes and bold cadmium orange panel.
2. **02 Product Triptych**: Three monumental devices side-by-side: Desktop Web App (Pillar Hub), Mobile Cashly App (Activity & Categories), Browser Companion (Checkout Detection).

6. **06 Desktop Experience**: Deep dive into the desktop dashboard, Safe to Spend calculations, and commitment feeds.
7. **07 Mobile Experience**: Cinematic mobile frames presenting the iOS/Android touch workflows.
8. **08 Five-Pillar Interactive System**: Home, Activity, Plan, Analyze, Assist with dynamic tab switching.
9. **09 Money Twin Engine**: Flagship predictive forecasting visual with interactive trajectory slider.
10. **10 Contextual AI / Assist**: Financial co-pilot generating concrete action chips from ledger anomalies.
11. **11 Browser Companion**: Full shopping scene featuring silent checkout capture on Amazon, Shopify, Foodpanda.
12. **12 Trust & Privacy Architecture**: Flowchart diagram of local-first capture and end-to-end ledger security.
13. **13 Final Campaign CTA**: Full-bleed Cadmium Orange climax block with massive uppercase typography.
14. **14 Minimal Footer**: Modern 4-column index with brand mark and legal links.

- **Hero Grid**: 3-column equal-flex layout with 44px phone geometry.
