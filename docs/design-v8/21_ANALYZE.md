# CASHLY V8 — PILLAR 4: FINANCIAL ANALYTICS & INTELLIGENCE

## 1. Intent: Structural Answers to Core Financial Questions

The Analyze pillar (`/analytics`, `/money-twin`, `/reports`) shifts the user mindset from historical record-keeping to **forward structural intelligence**.

It addresses:
- **Where is my cash actually draining?** (Spending Patterns)
- **If nothing changes, where will my month end?** (Money Twin Forecast)
- **Can I audit or export formal statements?** (Reports & Accounting Exports)

---

## 2. Navigation Architecture (`AnalyzeNavigationTabs.tsx`)

A unified tab segment connects the analysis modules:
- `Spending Patterns` (`/analytics`)
- `Money Twin & Forecast` (`/money-twin`)
- `Reports & Exports` (`/reports`)

Fully responsive with zero-collision tab items across all phone and tablet widths.
