## 1. Architectural Mission

The goal of Cashly V8 is a material, structural, and aesthetic leap forward from previous iterations:
- **From:** A warm-cream, card-heavy, rose-accented prototype feeling like a collection of generic dashboards.
- **To:** An elite, high-conviction **Modern Financial Operating System** (Deep Obsidian Ink, Crisp Pine Teal Accent, Architectural Surfaces, Tabular Precision, and Editorial Rhythm).

### Core Pillars
1. **HOME** (`/dashboard`): Immediate financial orientation with an authoritative **Safe Headroom** hero, attention rail, trajectory velocity, and upcoming commitments.
2. **ACTIVITY** (`/transactions`, `/transaction-inbox`, `/shopping-activity`): High-density financial ledger, zero fluff, instant review triage, and statement ingestion.
3. **PLAN** (`/budgets`, `/subscriptions`, `/goals`, `/cashflow-calendar`): Unified planning matrix—what is committed, what is safe to spend, what is being accumulated.
4. **ANALYZE** (`/analytics`, `/money-twin`, `/reports`): Direct visual answers to critical financial questions with predictive Monte Carlo simulation.
5. **ASSIST** (`/insights`, `/voice`): Neural Co-Pilot layer grounded in confirmed ledger data, with deep contextual action links.

### System Utilities
- **Cards & Accounts** (`/cards`): High-trust asset and liability management.
- **Extension Companion** (`/extension-health`): Browser automation telemetry and live capture monitor.
- **Settings & Workspace** (`/settings`): Six-zone unboxed financial control center.
- **Identity & Profile** (`/profile`): Credential and workspace identity.
