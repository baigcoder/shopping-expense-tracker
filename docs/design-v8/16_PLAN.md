# CASHLY V8 — PILLAR 3: FINANCIAL PLANNING SUITE

## 1. Unified Planning System

Pillar 3 (`/budgets`, `/subscriptions`, `/goals`, `/cashflow-calendar`) unites all future capital allocations into a single coherent system.

The user immediately understands:
1. **What is capped?** (Budgets & Limits)
2. **What is committed?** (Subscriptions & Recurring Bills)
3. **What is targeted?** (Savings Goals)
4. **What is the timeline?** (Cashflow Calendar)

---

## 2. Shared Pillar Sub-Navigation (`PlanNavigationTabs.tsx`)

A standardized horizontal pill bar connects all four planning disciplines:
- `Budgets & Limits` (`/budgets`)
- `Commitments & Bills` (`/subscriptions`)
- `Savings Goals` (`/goals`)
- `Cashflow Timeline` (`/cashflow-calendar`)

Equipped with `shrink-0` and `no-scrollbar` to guarantee smooth, zero-overlap scrolling on mobile devices (390px / 430px).
