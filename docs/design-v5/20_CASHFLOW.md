# CASHLY — CASHFLOW CALENDAR & TIMELINE (V5)

**Classification:** Feature Specification (Authority #20)  
**Primary Route:** `/cashflow-calendar`  
**Core Purpose:** Provide a monthly spatial timeline of cash inflows, bill due dates, and daily spending velocity.  

---

## 1. Visual Calendar Grid

- **7×6 Month Grid:** Clear day cells displaying date number, spending intensity background tint, and event chips.
- **Event Markers:**
  - `Inflow (+)`: Emerald pill (Payday, client deposits).
  - `Fixed Bill (▲)`: Amber pill indicating subscription/utility due date.
  - `Discretionary Peak (•)`: Rose marker on days with outsized purchase volume.
- **Interactive Day Drawer:** Clicking any calendar cell opens a slide-over schedule showing all transactions posted and obligations due on that exact day.
