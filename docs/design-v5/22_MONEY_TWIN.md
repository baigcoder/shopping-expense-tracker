# CASHLY — MONEY TWIN & PREDICTIVE FORECASTING (V5)

**Classification:** Flagship Mathematical Engine Specification (Authority #22)  
**Primary Route:** `/money-twin`  
**Core Purpose:** Answer *"If nothing changes, where will my month end?"* and model What-If scenarios.  

---

## 1. Flagship Narrative & Telemetry

Money Twin moves personal finance from historical bookkeeping to predictive clarity:
- **Headline:** *"If nothing changes, this is where your month ends."*
- **Daily Burn Velocity:** Real calculated average daily spend ($\text{Rs}/\text{day}$).
- **Cash Runway Headroom:** Exact number of days remaining before liquid cash reserves touch the safety buffer.
- **Predicted Month-End Balance:** Projected cash total accompanied by calibrated confidence intervals.

---

## 2. Interactive What-If Scenario Simulator

Users can manipulate interactive sliders to instantly test financial decisions:
1. **Reduce Category Spend:** e.g., cut Dining Out by 20% $\rightarrow$ shows immediate $+\text{Rs } 4,200$ buffer added this month.
2. **Cancel Subscriptions:** Toggle off 2 SaaS services $\rightarrow$ shows monthly cash savings and 3-year compound investment return.
3. **Income Adjustment:** Model a salary change or freelance bonus.
