# CASHLY — MASTER ART DIRECTION CHARTER (V5)

**Classification:** Visual & Artistic Master Charter (Authority #2)  
**Target Identity:** Premium Finance × Modern Software × Editorial Precision × Calm Intelligence  
**Status:** Canonical & Enforced  

---

## 1. Aesthetic Thesis & Visual Personality

Cashly is an executive-grade financial operating system designed for modern individuals and digital entrepreneurs who demand total clarity over their money.

- **Precise, Not Sterile:** Financial software must convey institutional certainty and mathematical rigor, balanced with warm natural surfaces that feel human.
- **Calm, Not Vacant:** The interface avoids both visual clutter and the opposite extreme of vast empty space devoid of functional utility. Every square pixel communicates financial state, meaning, or action.
- **Modern, Not Trendy:** Zero neobrutalist harsh black drop shadows, zero cartoon emojis in core financial tables, and zero neon AI gradients. Every interaction is grounded in quiet luxury and physical restraint.

---

## 2. Visual Tenets & Spatial Hierarchy

### 2.1 The 80/10/10 Color Distribution
- **80% Neutral Surfaces:** Warm canvas neutrals (`#F6F5F1`, `#FFFFFF`, `#EFEEE9`, `#DDDCD7`) provide the structural stage.
- **10% Deep Ink Typography:** Warm stone-black typography (`#171719` and `#56565C`) establishes clear reading hierarchy.
- **10% Semantic & Brand Accents:** Rose brand accent (`#D92F57`), Emerald health (`#167A52`), Amber attention (`#A35C00`), and Purple AI (`#7547C7`) are applied with discipline.

### 2.2 Surface Elevation & Hairline Structural Grid
- Replace repetitive boxed cards with an architectural panel structure.
- Primary boundaries are articulated using 1px hairline borders (`#DDDCD7` / dark: `#332E2A`).
- Elevation is purposeful:
  - `Base`: 0 elevation on open canvas.
  - `Surface`: 1px hairline border + subtle ambient shadow `0 1px 2px rgba(23, 23, 25, 0.04)`.
  - `Hover / Interactive`: Soft lift `0 8px 24px rgba(23, 23, 25, 0.06)`.
  - `Overlay / Side-Sheet`: Deep focus shadow `0 16px 40px rgba(23, 23, 25, 0.12)`.

### 2.3 Visual Rhythm & Asymmetry
- Avoid three identical consecutive sections on any page.
- Juxtapose high-density data tables against breathing analytical charts and contextual action modules.
- Utilize deliberate typographic scale shifts to guide the user's eye directly to what matters today.

---

## 3. Visual Anti-Patterns (Strictly Prohibited)

1. **Card Grid Monotony:** Never place 12 identical rounded rectangles in a 3×4 grid. Group related data into contextual panels or unified ledgers.
2. **Rose Overload:** Never make every badge, button, icon, and progress bar rose. Brand Rose is reserved for primary conversions, the Cashly monogram, and critical brand actions.
3. **Decorative Charts:** Never present a line or donut chart without axis markers, dates, units, and explicit mathematical meaning.
4. **Neon Gradients:** No bright glowing purple-to-cyan cyber gradients. AI interactions are framed in disciplined royal purple (`#7547C7`) with subtle lavender container fills (`#F1EBFF`).
5. **Mobile as Compressed Desktop:** Mobile must feature dedicated ergonomic navigation (bottom bar with 5 primary pillars, bottom-sheet drawers, and 44px+ tap targets).
