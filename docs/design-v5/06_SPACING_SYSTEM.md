## 2. Dynamic Spatial Rhythm (Non-Uniform Rule)

A foundational flaw of generic SaaS templates is utilizing identical 24px vertical padding between every single section. Cashly employs **deliberate spatial modulation**:

- **High-Density Data Pockets:** Inside the Activity Ledger and Review Inbox, spacing compresses to `8px`–`12px` to maximize rows per viewport and visual scan speed.
- **Cognitive Pause Pockets:** Around the Attention Rail and Financial Pulse, spacing expands to `32px`–`48px` to ensure critical alerts are not visually swallowed by surrounding data.
- **Editorial Pacing on Public Surfaces:** The Landing Page modulates between compressed product showcases (`48px`) and expansive editorial chapter breaks (`96px`–`120px`) to guide the reading rhythm.
