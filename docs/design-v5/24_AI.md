## 1. Grounded AI Interaction Model

Every AI insight generated in Cashly follows a strict 4-part structure:
1. **Observation:** What was detected in real financial data (*"Dining velocity is running 34% above your historical average"*).
2. **Context:** Why it matters (*"You have 2 committed utility bills due this Friday totaling Rs 4,600"*).
3. **Impact:** The calculated quantitative risk (*"Risk of exceeding discretionary monthly cap by Rs 6,300"*).
4. **Action:** Direct deep-linked action buttons:
   - `[Inspect Dining Spend]` $\rightarrow$ Opens `/transactions` with `category=Dining` filtered.
   - `[Protect Bill Headroom]` $\rightarrow$ Opens `/budgets`.
   - `[Ask Cashly]` $\rightarrow$ Opens conversational co-pilot with pre-filled query.

No AI card may exist as plain text without an actionable path.
