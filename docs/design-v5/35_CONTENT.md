# CASHLY — CONTENT & MICROCOPY DESIGN (V5)

**Classification:** Editorial & Content Design Specification (Authority #35)  
**System:** Cashly Financial Operating System  
**Status:** Canonical & Enforced  

---

## 1. Editorial Voice & Tone

Cashly's voice is that of an **intelligent, calm, highly capable financial co-pilot**:
- **Direct & Numeric:** State figures plainly. Replace *"Your spending is looking high this week"* with *"Dining is running 34% above your monthly average."*
- **Action-Oriented:** Pair every diagnostic with an immediate next step (*"Review 3 pending items"*, *"Protect Bill Headroom"*).
- **Prohibited Marketing Fluff:** Eliminate generic corporate buzzwords (*"Empower your fiscal future"*, *"Revolutionize your spending habits"*).

---

## 2. Empty State Copy Standards

| Context | Bad Copy | Cashly Standard Copy | Direct Action CTA |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Review Inbox** | "No transactions found." | "Inbox is quiet. All captured purchases have been approved to your ledger." | `[Upload Bank Statement]` |
| **Commitments** | "You have no recurring bills." | "No commitments tracked yet. Add fixed obligations like rent and subscriptions to calculate your Safe Headroom." | `[+ Track First Commitment]` |
| **Goals** | "No goals created." | "No active savings targets. Set a target amount and date to track your required monthly pace." | `[+ Create Savings Goal]` |
