# CASHLY V9 — 14 PILLAR 4: ANALYZE & MONEY TWIN
## Immersive Analytical Instrument & Predictive Simulation

### 1. The Immersive Analytical Surface

Analytics switches into an intentional dark-canvas optical environment (`#09141B` / `#101E25`) to maximize data contrast and reduce glare:
- **Quiet Axes & High-Fidelity Curves**: Data visualization minimizes chart grid clutter; numbers and trend lines speak with clarity.
- **Question-Driven Visualizations**: Every chart answers an explicit operational question:
  - *Where is capital accelerating?* → Category velocity delta chart.
  - *Are inflows covering fixed commitments?* → Net operational margin ribbon.

### 2. Money Twin Signature Experience

Money Twin is Cashly's flagship predictive modeling engine:
- Full-width continuous simulation canvas.
- Multi-vector trajectory displaying actual historical balance + 90-day probabilistic forecast with Safe and Risk confidence corridors.
- Interactive "What-If" scenario sliders directly integrated beneath the trajectory (e.g., discretionary cut, salary adjustment, planned large expenditure).
- Runway and liquidation countdowns computed dynamically.
