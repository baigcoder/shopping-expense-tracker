# CASHLY V9 — 12 PILLAR 2: ACTIVITY & REVIEW
## High-Density Operational Workspace

### 1. Unified Ledger & Decision Workspace

Activity rejects the card-stack paradigm. Ledger management is a high-volume financial process demanding dense, perfectly aligned tabular presentation:
- **Search & Filter Control Bar**: Fluid search bar with category pills, date range scrubbers, and export actions.
- **High-Density Ledger Table**:
  - Merchant identity with verified favicon/category glyph.
  - Category badge in muted neutral tones with subtle semantic dot.
  - Tabular amount in bold (`-Rs 1,450.00` in Dark Ink / Inflow in Green).
  - Status indicator: Verified / Pending / Flagged.
  - Direct row inspection trigger sliding the right-hand inspection sheet.
- **Review Inbox Workspace**:
  - High-focus decision surface for unverified transactions.
  - Displays primary pending candidate with full merchant, date, account, and AI confidence score.
  - Instant keyboard hotkeys (`A` for Approve, `E` for Edit, `D` for Dismiss).
  - Batch approval bar for high-throughput clearing.
