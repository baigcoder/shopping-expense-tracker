# CASHLY V9 — 13 PILLAR 3: PLAN
## The Financial Planning Desk

### 1. Unified Planning Desk Architecture

Plan integrates Budgets, Commitments (Subscriptions/Bills), Goals, and Cashflow Forecasting into a singular coherent desk:
- **Velocity Spectrum (Budget Limits)**:
  - Visual allocation ribbon showing total monthly ceiling vs actual burn rate.
  - Category rows organized by velocity priority (Nearing limit at top in warm amber).
- **Commitments Horizon (Recurring Subscriptions & Bills)**:
  - Timeline horizon view mapping upcoming scheduled obligations over the next 30 days.
  - Active trial countdown sentinel flags.
- **Goal Trajectory & Savings Horizon**:
  - Trajectory vectors tracking goal targets against real-time deposit velocity.
