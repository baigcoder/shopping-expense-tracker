# CASHLY V9 — 11 PILLAR 1: HOME
## The Primary Financial Canvas

### 1. Compositional Architecture

Home is the operational nerve center. It strictly avoids repeating KPI cards:
1. **Primary Financial Monument (Hero Anchor)**:
   - "SAFE TO SPEND": Dominating 56px–64px tabular numeral figure (`Rs 42,870`).
   - Supporting context beneath: Liquid Cash (`Rs 142,500`), Committed Bills (`-Rs 48,200`), Planned Expenses (`-Rs 51,430`).
   - Continuous mathematical velocity bar directly below the number.
2. **Attention & Action Rail**:
   - High-priority operational triage ribbon: Pending extension captures, approaching category thresholds, or subscription renewals.
3. **Cash Trajectory & Velocity Canvas**:
   - Open graph surface detailing 30-day inflow vs outflow rhythm without enclosing borders.
4. **Active Commitments & Recent Decision Stream**:
   - Tabular stream of upcoming debits and recent entries with inline review triggers.
