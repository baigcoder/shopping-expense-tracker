### 2. Semantic Discipline Rules

1. **Brand Identity is NOT Financial Gain**: Sovereign Jade (#0E8174 / #16A394) is strictly Cashly brand identity, primary navigation anchors, and primary workflow triggers (e.g., "Confirm Transaction", "Run Simulation"). Income and positive yield MUST use Forest Green (#17824F).
2. **Amber is for Velocity & Sentinel Alerts**: Never use amber as a decorative badge. It signals thresholds approaching (>80% of budget, unapproved recurring subscriptions, trial expiration in <48h).
3. **Violet is Dedicated to AI**: AI predictions, auto-categorizations, voice recognition waveforms, and autonomous actions are tinted with Sovereign Violet (#7753C7). Never mix violet with standard metrics.
4. **Accessible Contrast Ratios**: All text against background meets or exceeds WCAG 2.2 AA (4.5:1 for body copy, 3:1 for large display numerals).
