## 1. The Core Commandment

> **Do not make Cashly look like another SaaS company. Make Cashly look like CASHLY.**

The public website is the front door to the **5-Pillar Financial Operating System** (`Home`, `Activity`, `Plan`, `Analyze`, `Assist`). The visitor must leave the landing page understanding:

1. **Cashly captures what happens** — silently from the browser while shopping, without demanding bank passwords.
2. **You stay in control of what becomes real** — purchases wait in a quiet inbox until approved.
3. **Cashly helps you understand** — with deep, multi-dimensional ledger analytics.
4. **Cashly helps you plan** — by unifying subscriptions, bills, and budgets into your true discretionary headroom.
5. **Cashly shows you what comes next** — with the Money Twin forecasting your month-end cash.
6. **Cashly's AI helps you act** — through pattern alerts and direct, clickable action buttons.

---

## 2. Inviolable Guardrails

1. **Ground Everything in the Real Product:**
   - Every screenshot, widget, metric, or chart shown on the public site must represent the actual authenticated product. Never fabricate fictional features.
2. **Never Break Authentication Logic:**
   - Preserve Supabase Auth, OAuth callbacks, OTP verification, and error formatting.
3. **Calm Finance Consistency:**
   - The public site, login screen, and authenticated dashboard must feel like the exact same software product.
4. **Performance & Accessibility First:**
   - Zero heavyweight animation libraries, full WCAG 2.2 AA compliance, and lightning-fast load times.
