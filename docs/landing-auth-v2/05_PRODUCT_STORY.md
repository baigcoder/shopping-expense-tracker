## 1. The Core Truth

> **Your money is happening all the time.**  
> Online purchases, monthly renewals, grocery runs, delivery fees, and split bills happen across dozens of apps, cards, and browser tabs.

Most personal finance tools force you into one of two extremes:
1. **Manual Spreadsheets & Loggers:** Exhausting, friction-filled manual data entry that you abandon by the second week of the month.
2. **Aggressive Plaid Scrapers:** Opaque banking aggregators that ask for your bank password, lag by 3 to 5 days, misclassify Amazon packages as "Merchandise", and dump hundreds of unvetted charges directly into your balance.

---

## 2. The Cashly Difference: Human-in-the-Loop Financial OS

Cashly introduces a third, superior paradigm:

```
[ CAPTURE ] ──► [ REVIEW ] ──► [ UNDERSTAND ] ──► [ PLAN ] ──► [ PREDICT ] ──► [ ACT ]
  Silently        You Decide        Real Ledger      Commitments     Money Twin       Contextual
  while you shop    What is Real    & Deep Analytics   & Budgets      Runway Pace      AI Action Chips
```

### 1. CAPTURE
Cashly’s lightweight Chrome companion notices checkouts and subscription signups right in your browser as you shop. It never asks for your online banking password. It catches the exact merchant, line items, and amount in real time.

### 2. REVIEW (The Core Moat)
Nothing enters your financial ledger until you approve it. Transactions wait quietly in your **Needs Review** inbox. You can approve with one click, adjust a category, split an item, merge a duplicate, or reject it entirely.

### 3. UNDERSTAND (Canonical Ledger & Analytics)
Once approved, transactions post to your authoritative ledger. Analytics break down exactly where your money went, comparing online spending vs in-store POS, category velocity curves, and top merchants.

### 4. PLAN (Unified Commitments & Headroom)
Instead of scattering Netflix, rent, electric bills, and free trials across 4 separate menus, Cashly unifies them into **Plan > Commitments**. You always know your **Discretionary Net Headroom**: *what money is actually yours to spend after all obligations are spoken for.*

### 5. PREDICT (Money Twin)
*"If nothing changes, this is where your month ends."* The Money Twin calculates your real daily burn rate and projects your month-end cash balance, warning you of trajectory risks before they happen.

### 6. ACT (Contextual AI Co-Pilot)
AI is not a generic chatbot reciting finance trivia. Cashly's AI spots real patterns (*"Dining is $240 above your normal pace"*) and generates direct, structured action buttons (`[View Dining Spend]`, `[Adjust Budget]`, `[Review 3 Items]`).
